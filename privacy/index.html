Privacy Policy

Origami Zen · Last updated October 6, 2026

Origami Zen is a single-player puzzle game made by Tinh Ngo ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

We do not collect data

Origami Zen does not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no in-app purchases, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (scores, saved game, paper balance, themes, daily progress, sound, haptics and language) are saved only on your device. They never leave it, and deleting the app removes them.

Internet access

The game works fully offline. The only time it opens the internet is when you tap the Privacy policy link in Settings, which opens this page in your browser.

Children

Origami Zen is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes — for example if a future version adds optional features that use data — we will update this page and the date above before that version is released.
