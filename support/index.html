How to play

  • Drag a block from the tray onto the board, or tap a block and then tap a cell.
  • Fill a whole row, column or 3×3 area to clear it.
  • Clear three times in a row to charge Zen Fever, tap Unfold and pick a 3×3 area for double points.
  • When no block fits, you can revive once per game or use a hammer to break a cell.
  • Daily Origami tasks unlock folds of a paper artwork; finished works appear in the Paper Garden.

Common questions

Is my progress saved? Yes, automatically after every move, on your device.

Does it need internet? No, Origami Zen works fully offline.

How do I change the language? Settings → Language (English / Tiếng Việt).
